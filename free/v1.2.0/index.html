Made by Bu Khalil Studio

Azmeel is designed and built by Bu Khalil Studio, the creative studio of designer Ibrahim Khalil. The studio works on brand identity and visual design, and made Azmeel to bring hand-engraved, halftone and print-grain looks to Illustrator as clean, editable vectors.
